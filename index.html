Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what our customers often ask.

Do you repair both old and new CNC machines?
Absolutely. We specialise in legacy systems (CRT, older controllers) but also handle the latest CNC equipment. We have spares for many obsolete models.
How long does a typical repair take?
Most monitor or board repairs are completed within 2–3 working days. Major spindle or drive repairs may take 4–5 days. We always provide a timeline upfront.
Do you offer on‑site service?
Yes, we provide on‑site support for installations, diagnostics, and large equipment. Our workshop is also fully equipped for complex component‑level repairs.
What are your rates like compared to others?
We offer competitive, transparent pricing. Because we repair at component level, we avoid costly full‑board replacements, saving you 30–50% compared to typical repair shops.
